Budget Wheel

Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Budget Wheel is an on-device budgeting app. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or financial information.

What the app stores

Income estimates, budget categories, purchases, and spending history stay on the device where you installed Budget Wheel. That information is written to local app storage so the app and the home-screen widget can show the same budget.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email, name, or password
  • No bank, card, or payment-account connections
  • No advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access
  • No upload to any server operated by us or a third party

Internet

The Android app does not request the INTERNET permission. The app and widget work offline. History PDFs you export are saved on your device.

Backups and uninstall

Cloud backup of app data is turned off. If you uninstall Budget Wheel or erase the device, the budget on that device cannot be recovered unless you already exported a PDF from Spending history.

Children

Budget Wheel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change. Because the app does not collect contact information, we cannot email you about updates.
