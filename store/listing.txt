Full description
================================================================
Budget Wheel is hands-on financial planning without a bank login. You log income and expenses yourself. Nothing is imported, and your data stays on this device.

See where every dollar of take-home pay goes on a live spending wheel. Each slice is a category. Tap a slice to review it, add a purchase, or tidy the name and color. Prefer exact bars? Switch to a graph of the same month.

Start with real income. Choose salary or hourly, add your state, and get a monthly take-home estimate you can confirm or adjust. Add a second income, side hustle, or one-off payday in Settings so the wheel knows what you actually have to spend.

Give each category a dollar amount or a percent of income. Anything you do not assign becomes Extra Funds. Log spending with I purchased. Spending starts over on the 1st.

Pin a home-screen widget — compact 2×2 or wider 4×2 — to see what’s left this month without opening the app. The widget is a $1.99 one-time unlock. Dark is the default; Light and System live in Settings.

After a month ends, download your wheel: a picture plus a CSV you own. Files stay on the device unless you share them. Closed months, quarters, and years stay in Spending history.

Your information stays on this device. Budget Wheel does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal or financial information. There is no account and no server. Uninstalling the app erases the budget on that device.

Take-home is an estimate for a single filer with no local tax. It is a planning number, not tax advice.
