Full description
================================================================
Budget Wheel shows where every dollar of your take-home pay goes.

Start with real income. Choose salary or hourly, add your state, and get a monthly take-home estimate you can confirm or adjust. Then give each category a dollar amount or a percent of income. Anything you do not assign becomes Extra Funds.

The home screen is a wheel. 100% is this month’s income. Tap a slice to see what you spent. Tap I purchased, enter the amount, and pick a category. If a slice is already selected, the purchase goes straight there.

Need a clearer view? Switch between the wheel and a bar graph. Change Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly without rewriting the budget you set.

The home-screen widget matches the app. It shows this month’s wheel or graph and lets you log a purchase without opening Budget Wheel. The app and widget stay in sync.

Spending starts over on the 1st. Closed months, quarters, and years stay in Spending history on this device. Download a PDF or delete history when you want.

Your information stays on this device. Budget Wheel does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal or financial information. There is no account and no server. Uninstalling the app erases the budget on that device.

Take-home is an estimate for a single filer with no local tax. It is a planning number, not tax advice.
